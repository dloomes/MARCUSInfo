**Roadmap** shows what Involve plans to add to M.A.R.C.U.S. over the next twelve months, quarter by quarter. Go to **Roadmap** in the sidebar. Everyone who can sign in to the portal can see it, whatever their role.

## How to read it

The roadmap is laid out as a grid. The columns are quarters, starting with **Now**, the current quarter, which is highlighted. The rows are themes, such as **Faster onboarding** or **Device coverage**. Each card is one feature, with a short description of what it gives you.

Each card has a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Committed** | It's planned for a named monthly release, shown on the card, such as **Jan release**. |
| **Planned** | It's designed and scheduled for that quarter, but the quarter may move. |
| **Exploring** | We're shaping it with customers. The timing is our current best guess. |

On a phone, the roadmap is shown as a list for each quarter.

Below the grid, **Recently shipped** lists what has gone live in recent releases.

The roadmap shows our current plans. **Planned** and **Exploring** items, and their timing, may change as we learn from customers. Please don't rely on it for purchasing decisions. If a feature matters to you, tell your Involve account manager.

## Sharing the roadmap

If someone in your organisation can't sign in to the portal but needs to see the roadmap, ask your Involve account manager. Involve can send them a personal link that opens a read-only copy of the roadmap, valid for a set period.
